# Contoso Leave Policy

> **Synthetic training data for AB-100 Module 1 labs.** This document is
> fictional and is designed only for demonstrating grounded AI-agent
> behavior. It is not an actual Microsoft or Contoso HR policy.

**Document owner:** Contoso Human Resources\
**Policy version:** 1.0\
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026\
**Review cycle:** Annual

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the leave categories used in the AB-100 Employee
Service Agent training scenario. Employees should use the HR service
desk for exceptions or cases not covered by this document.

## 2. Annual Leave

Eligible full-time employees receive **20 working days of annual leave
per calendar year**.

Annual leave should normally be requested in advance through the
approved leave-request process. Approval depends on business coverage
and manager approval.

### Carry-forward

Employees may carry forward **up to 5 unused annual-leave days** into
the next calendar year.

Carried-forward annual leave must be used by **March 31** of the
following year. Unused carried-forward days expire after that date
unless HR has approved an exception.

> Carry-forward applies to **annual leave only**. Other leave categories
> have separate rules.

## 3. Marriage Leave

Eligible employees may take **3 working days of paid marriage leave**
for their own marriage.

The employee should submit:

-   the requested leave dates,
-   manager approval, and
-   supporting documentation requested by HR.

Marriage leave should normally be taken within **30 calendar days before
or after the marriage date**.

For exceptions, the employee should contact HR.

## 4. Parental Leave

Eligible employees may take **12 weeks of paid parental leave**
following the birth or adoption of a child.

Parental leave should be coordinated with HR and the employee's manager
before the planned start date where reasonably possible.

Employees should provide the documentation requested by HR for the
applicable birth or adoption event.

This training policy does not define country-specific statutory
entitlements. If a statutory entitlement differs from this training
policy, the case must be escalated to HR.

## 5. Sick Leave

Employees may use sick leave when they are unable to work because of
illness or injury.

For an absence of **more than 3 consecutive working days**, the employee
must contact HR regarding any supporting documentation that may be
required.

The Employee Service Agent must not provide medical diagnosis or medical
advice.

## 6. Bereavement Leave

Eligible employees may take **up to 5 working days of paid bereavement
leave** following the death of an immediate family member.

Cases requiring additional time should be escalated to HR.

## 7. Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave is not automatically approved.

Requests for unpaid leave require:

1.  manager review, and
2.  HR approval.

The Employee Service Agent may explain this process but must not promise
approval.

## 8. Questions the Agent Should Clarify

If an employee asks:

> Can I carry my leave?

The agent should clarify that the employee means **annual leave**,
because carry-forward rules in this document apply specifically to
annual leave.

If an employee asks about a leave category not defined here, the agent
should state that the available policy does not contain enough
information and direct the employee to HR.

## 9. Future Policy

This document defines policy only for its stated effective period.

The agent must **not predict future leave policy**.

For example, if asked:

> What will the 2027 parental leave policy be?

the agent should explain that the available policy does not establish
the 2027 policy and recommend checking with HR or an updated
authoritative source.

## 10. Escalation

Escalate to HR when:

-   policy information is missing,
-   two authoritative sources conflict,
-   an employee requests an exception,
-   statutory or country-specific rules may apply,
-   the employee disputes how the policy applies to them,
-   the request involves sensitive circumstances not appropriate for
    automated resolution.
