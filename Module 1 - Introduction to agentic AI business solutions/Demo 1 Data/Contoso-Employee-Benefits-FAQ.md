# Contoso Employee Benefits FAQ

> **Synthetic training data for AB-100 Module 1 labs.** This FAQ is
> fictional and should not be treated as an actual employee-benefits
> plan or Microsoft policy.

**Document owner:** Contoso Human Resources\
**FAQ version:** 1.0\
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026

## 1. What is this FAQ for?

This document provides sample employee-benefit information for testing
the AB-100 Employee Service Agent.

It intentionally contains clear answers, scope boundaries, and
escalation cases so learners can evaluate grounded agent behavior.

## 2. Who is eligible for the benefits described here?

The benefits in this training FAQ apply to **eligible full-time
employees** unless a section says otherwise.

Eligibility for contractors, interns, part-time employees, temporary
workers, or country-specific programs is **not defined in this FAQ**.
Those questions must be escalated to HR.

## 3. When does benefits coverage begin?

For this training scenario, eligible employee benefits become active on
the employee's **first day of employment**, subject to completion of any
required enrollment steps.

## 4. How long do I have to complete enrollment?

New employees should complete required benefits enrollment within **30
calendar days of their start date**.

If the employee misses the enrollment window, the agent should direct
the employee to HR rather than promise that late enrollment will be
accepted.

## 5. Does Contoso provide health coverage?

Yes. In this synthetic training scenario, eligible full-time employees
may enroll in a company health plan.

Specific providers, premiums, deductibles, coverage levels, and
country-specific terms are intentionally **not included** in this FAQ.

The agent must not invent them.

## 6. Does Contoso provide life insurance?

For this training scenario, eligible full-time employees receive a
**basic employer-paid life-insurance benefit**.

The amount of coverage and optional supplemental coverage are not
defined here. Questions about those details should be escalated to HR.

## 7. Is there a retirement or savings benefit?

The training scenario includes an employer-supported retirement/savings
program for eligible employees.

Contribution percentages, matching formulas, investment options, tax
treatment, and country-specific rules are deliberately omitted.

The Employee Service Agent must not provide investment advice.

## 8. Are learning and development benefits available?

Eligible employees may access approved internal learning resources and
may request manager approval for role-relevant external learning.

The FAQ does not guarantee reimbursement for a particular course,
certification, conference, or degree.

## 9. Is home-office equipment a benefit?

Standard company equipment required for work is handled through the
approved IT/equipment process and should not be described as a cash
employee benefit.

Employees should not purchase non-standard equipment and assume
reimbursement.

## 10. Can I add family members to my health plan?

Dependent eligibility may vary by plan and location.

This FAQ does not define dependent eligibility. The agent should direct
the employee to the authoritative benefits enrollment information or HR.

## 11. Can the agent recommend which benefit option I should select?

No.

The agent may explain factual information contained in approved benefits
sources, but it should not provide personalized:

-   financial advice,
-   investment advice,
-   legal advice,
-   medical advice.

## 12. What if two benefits documents disagree?

The agent should not choose a policy based on guesswork.

It should:

1.  explain that conflicting information was found,
2.  avoid presenting either version as definitively correct unless
    source authority is established,
3.  direct the employee to HR or the authoritative benefits source.

## 13. What if I ask about next year's benefits?

The agent should not predict future benefits.

Example:

> Will the company increase retirement matching next year?

Expected behavior:

> The configured benefits information does not establish next year's
> matching terms. Please check the latest authoritative benefits
> information or contact HR.

## 14. Privacy

The Employee Service Agent should not reveal another employee's:

-   benefit selections,
-   dependent information,
-   compensation,
-   health information,
-   personal contact information.

Requests for personal benefit records should use authenticated,
authorized systems rather than general knowledge responses.

## 15. When should the agent escalate?

Escalate when:

-   employee eligibility is unclear,
-   country-specific rules are required,
-   the employee missed an enrollment deadline,
-   plan-specific details are absent,
-   personalized financial/legal/medical advice is requested,
-   documents conflict,
-   the employee requests another person's information,
-   the configured knowledge does not contain the answer.
