# Contoso Employee Onboarding Checklist

> **Synthetic training data for AB-100 Module 1 labs.** This document is
> fictional and exists only for the Employee Service Agent
> demonstration.

**Document owner:** Contoso Human Resources and IT\
**Checklist version:** 1.0\
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This checklist provides a simple authoritative source for the AB-100
onboarding-agent exercises.

## 2. Before Your First Day

New employees should complete the following activities when the required
information or invitation has been provided:

-   Review and electronically acknowledge the employment documents sent
    by HR.
-   Confirm personal contact information in the onboarding portal.
-   Complete required identity-verification steps.
-   Review the first-day schedule sent by the hiring manager or HR.
-   Confirm whether the role is office-based, remote, or hybrid.
-   Complete any mandatory pre-start compliance training assigned by HR.
-   Contact the onboarding team if required credentials or instructions
    have not arrived.

## 3. Equipment

For roles that require company equipment:

-   Standard equipment is arranged through the approved IT onboarding
    process.
-   Employees should not independently purchase equipment and assume
    reimbursement.
-   Missing or incorrect equipment should be reported through the IT
    service process.
-   Non-standard equipment may require manager and/or IT approval.

## 4. First-Day Checklist

On the first working day, the employee should:

1.  Sign in using the company identity provided through the approved
    onboarding process.
2.  Complete any remaining security-registration steps.
3.  Confirm access to required collaboration tools.
4.  Meet the hiring manager or designated onboarding contact.
5.  Review team objectives and the initial work plan.
6.  Complete assigned security and compliance training.
7.  Report missing system access through the approved support process.

## 5. First-Week Checklist

During the first week, the employee should:

-   review applicable HR policies,
-   complete mandatory training,
-   confirm access to role-specific systems,
-   meet key team members and stakeholders,
-   review performance expectations with the manager,
-   raise access or equipment issues through the appropriate service
    channel.

## 6. Security Guidance

New employees must:

-   use only their assigned identity,
-   never share passwords or authentication codes,
-   follow company multi-factor authentication requirements,
-   report suspicious sign-in or phishing activity through the approved
    security channel.

The Employee Service Agent must never ask an employee to provide a
password or authentication code in chat.

## 7. Example Grounded Question

Employee:

> I'm joining Contoso next Monday. What should I complete before my
> first day?

Expected agent behavior:

-   summarize the **Before Your First Day** section,
-   keep the answer grounded in this checklist,
-   explain that specific schedules or credentials depend on information
    issued by HR/IT,
-   avoid inventing role-specific requirements.

## 8. Missing Information

This checklist intentionally does **not** define:

-   a universal first-day start time,
-   a universal office location,
-   a specific hiring manager,
-   an employee's username or password,
-   country-specific employment requirements.

The agent must not invent these details.

## 9. Escalation

Direct the employee to the onboarding/HR or IT support process when:

-   credentials have not arrived,
-   identity verification cannot be completed,
-   equipment is missing,
-   required system access is unavailable,
-   the employee needs a country-specific requirement not covered here,
-   the employee asks for information that is not contained in the
    configured knowledge.
