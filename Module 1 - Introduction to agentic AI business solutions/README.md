# AB-100 Module 1 --- Agentic AI Demo & Use-Case Lab Pack

## Module

**Introduction to Agentic AI Business Solution Architecture**

## Purpose

This pack converts the Module 1 concepts into a continuous,
trainer-ready practical storyline. It contains:

-   Two detailed hands-on agentic demo lab guides.
-   Six detailed unit-aligned use-case guides.
-   One end-to-end case study spanning the module.
-   Suggested timing, learner tasks, architecture decisions, test cases,
    expected outcomes, and debrief questions.

## Recommended continuous scenario

**Contoso Employee Service Transformation**

Contoso has a large employee population and HR/IT teams receive
repetitive requests about leave, benefits, onboarding, policies, travel,
equipment, and support. The organization wants to introduce an Employee
Service Agent that can answer grounded questions, guide employees
through processes, invoke governed actions, and escalate exceptions to
humans.

> **Training note:** The organization name, volumes, targets, sample
> policies, ticket IDs, and other operational details in this lab pack
> are synthetic training content created for the exercises. They are not
> claims made by the source deck.

## Recommended learning progression

1.  Introduction --- identify the business outcome and candidate agent.
2.  Role of the Architect in AI Transformation --- make architecture and
    governance decisions.
3.  Overview of Microsoft AI Technologies --- map requirements to
    Microsoft AI capabilities.
4.  Identify OOB Microsoft AI Agent Resources --- make
    build/reuse/extend decisions.
5.  Identify OOB Microsoft AI Agents --- build and customize a grounded
    agent.
6.  Knowledge Checks / consolidation --- evolve the solution into a
    governed agentic workflow.
7.  Capstone --- review the complete architecture, KPIs, risks, and
    human oversight.
