# Case Study --- Contoso Customer Operations Intelligence

## Scope

Entire AB-100 Module 2. \## Situation Contoso wants agents to reduce
repetitive support work, analyze service patterns, and help managers
make better operational decisions. Its enterprise data includes good
sources but also stale, duplicate, conflicting, irrelevant, and
restricted content. \## Stage 1 --- Assess agent value Prioritize task
automation, analytics, decision support, and knowledge retrieval.
Output: **Use-Case Prioritization Matrix**. \## Stage 2 --- Analyze
operations Run Demo 1. Summarize support data, find patterns, and
recommend evidence-backed next actions while retaining human judgment.
\## Stage 3 --- Diagnose grounding A manager reports inconsistent
Priority 1 targets. Evaluate all candidate sources across Accuracy,
Relevance, Timeliness, Cleanliness, and Availability. \## Stage 4 ---
Curate and retest Run Demo 2. Remove/remediate weak sources and retest
the same questions. \## Stage 5 --- Organize data for AI

``` text
CRM / ERP / Files / Databases
             |
   Ingestion + Transformation
             |
      Curated Data Hub
             |
       Governance Layer
             |
    Semantic / Retrieval
             |
      +------+------+
      |             |
    Agents      Other AI Apps
```

## Stage 6 --- Steering committee

Present: business outcome, chosen use cases, grounding findings,
remediation, target data architecture, governance, human decision
boundaries, and KPIs. \## Suggested rubric \| Area \| Weight \|
