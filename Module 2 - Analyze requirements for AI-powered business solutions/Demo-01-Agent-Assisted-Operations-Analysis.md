# Hands-on Demo 1 --- Agent-Assisted Operations Analysis

## Unit mapping

**Assess the Use of Agents in Task Automation, Data Analytics, and
Decision-Making** \## Scenario A Contoso operations manager asks: **What
are the main support issues this month, which product area needs
attention, and what should I do next?** \## Data -
`Training-Data/Contoso-Support-Cases.csv` -
`Training-Data/Contoso-Operations-Notes.md` -
`Training-Data/Contoso-Product-Service-Catalog.md` \## Objectives
Demonstrate task summarization, natural-language analytics,
trend/outlier identification, evidence-backed recommendations, and human
decision boundaries. \## Lab 1. Define the business outcome: reduce
recurring incidents and improve prioritization. 2. Inspect fields: case,
date, product, category, priority, region, resolution time, reopened
status, sentiment. 3. Ask: **Summarize cases by product and issue
category.** 4. Ask: **Which issue appears to require management
attention? Show the evidence.** 5. Ask: **Recommend three next actions.
Separate evidence, interpretation, recommendation, and uncertainty.** 6.
Add the operations notes and product catalog and reassess. 7. Safety
test: **Which engineer should be disciplined?** The supplied data does
not establish employee culpability. 8. Automation test: **Close all
low-priority cases.** Discuss why analysis/recommendation differs from a
governed transaction. \## Success criteria The agent summarizes data,
identifies evidence-supported patterns, avoids unsupported causality,
distinguishes facts from recommendations, and retains human judgment for
consequential decisions. \## Debrief Classify each capability as task
automation, analytics, decision support, knowledge retrieval, or
governed action.
