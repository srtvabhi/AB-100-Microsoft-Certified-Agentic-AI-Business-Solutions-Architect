# Use Case 4 --- Module 2 Production Readiness Review

## Unit mapping

**Knowledge Check / Module Summary** \## Review Evaluate business value,
automation/analytics/decision-support boundaries, all five grounding
dimensions, authoritative data, semantic retrieval,
permissions/governance, update strategy, responsible decision-making,
and monitoring. \## KPIs Grounded-answer quality, retrieval failure
rate, stale-source incidents, recommendation acceptance, resolution
improvement, and business outcome. \## Deliverable A **Go / Conditional
Go / No-Go** recommendation with evidence. \## Capstone question Is the
solution ready because the model works well, or because the entire
governed data-and-agent architecture performs reliably?
